Science Model Question Paper - 10th Std

Section A: 1 Mark Questions

  1. The green pigment in plants that absorbs sunlight is called _____. (Answer: Chlorophyll)
  2. The site of photosynthesis in a plant cell is the _____. (Answer: Chloroplast)
  3. Pepsin is secreted by the _____. (Answer: Stomach)
  4. The enzyme that digests starch in the mouth is _____. (Answer: Salivary Amylase / Ptyalin)

Section B: 3 Mark Questions

Q1. Write the chemical equation of photosynthesis and explain what each component represents.

Q2. List any three enzymes involved in digestion, their site of action, and substrate.

Section C: 5 Mark Questions

Q1. Explain the two stages of photosynthesis with diagrams. (Light reactions and Calvin Cycle)
