Important Formulas

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
a² - b² = (a + b)(a - b)

Quick Tips

  • Always simplify like terms first.
  • Check signs while expanding equations.
  • Practice factorization daily.
  • Verify answers by substitution.

Example

Simplify: 2x + 3x - 4

Answer: 5x - 4
